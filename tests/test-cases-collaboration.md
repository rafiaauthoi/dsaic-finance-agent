# Rules Checker Test Cases: Collaboration Proposals

This test set covers COL-01 through COL-05 from section 4.4, [Collaboration funding in detail][collaboration-rules], in the canonical WSA rules reference. It uses the five-column format of [test-cases-event.md](test-cases-event.md).

Each passing example should produce no violation of the named rule, and each failing example should produce the message in the last column. Evaluate labeled subcases separately. Examples are self-contained; other applicable rules may produce additional warnings.

All clubs, people, vendors, dates, records, and scenario amounts are fictional. Policy limits come from the reference unless explicitly labeled as a test fixture. Assume all other eligibility and submission requirements are met. **Manual check** identifies facts that cannot be established from the proposal sheet alone. The external evidence described in a case is fictional test input, not something the checker may infer from the sheet; a person must verify it before deciding.

## Notes for reviewing the cases

- **COL-02:** Attendance is a deliberation fact, not something a completed proposal or typed signature can prove.
- **COL-03:** Verify each partner's purchasing responsibility and amount without double-counting a shared expense. Separate proposals do not substitute for the required shared collaboration budget.
- **COL-04:** The $1,500 limit is per RSO per fiscal year, not per event and not one pooled allowance. The $6,500 non-collaboration cap is separate.
- **COL-05:** The subcases illustrate inherited Event restrictions. For broader inherited coverage, also evaluate the EVT-01 through EVT-17 cases in [test-cases-event.md](test-cases-event.md) with an otherwise valid two-RSO collaboration setup, complete shared budget and signatures, attending representatives, and sufficient remaining collaboration allocations. Preserve each Event case's cap, banned-item, and manual-check expectations.
- **Timing fixture:** Count weekdays after the fictional deliberation on 03/01/2027, excluding weekends and assuming no university holidays or closures. The event dates 03/12/2027 and 03/15/2027 are business days 9 and 10 respectively. A real decision needs the actual university calendar.
