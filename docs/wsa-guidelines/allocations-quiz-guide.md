# WSAAC Allocations Quiz: Study Guide (2026-27)

**Before a club can submit any funding request, its allocations representative must score 80% or higher on WSAAC's Final Allocations Quiz.** The quiz has to be retaken every school year, and again whenever WSAAC changes the bylaws, even if the rep already passed that year. Until the rep passes, the club's submission dropbox stays closed.

The quiz is open book: keep the Step 1 to 7 slides and the current bylaws open while you take it. This guide covers the topics the quiz tests so you know where to look. It is not an answer key. Rule IDs point to [wsa-club-finance-domain.md](wsa-club-finance-domain.md).

| Topic | What to know | Rule IDs |
|---|---|---|
| **The basics** | WSAAC is the WSA Allocations Commission and decides funding proposal by proposal.<br>The money comes from the Student Assessment Fee (SAF) every student pays each semester.<br>A club only has funding once it receives a funding letter. Nothing bought before that is covered. | MON-04, CYC-08 |
| **Who can apply** | Registered with OSE, and owes no money to any WMU office.<br>A representative in good standing with the WSA Assembly (Intent to Represent form; more than 2 unexcused absences means bad standing).<br>The rep scores 80% or higher on the current quiz. One person can represent only one club per cycle.<br>Every requirement must already be met on the day you submit. | ELG-01 to ELG-20 |
| **Caps** | $6,500 per club per fiscal year (July 1 to June 30) for everything except collaborations.<br>$1,500 per club per year for collaborations, separate from the $6,500.<br>Start-up: $500 for clubs one semester old or less. Bronco Funding is WSAAC's own; clubs cannot request it. | COL-04, OTH-01, OTH-02 |
| **Budget types and food** | Event: open and free to students, food on campus only (up to $20 per expected attendee), off-campus events capped at $500, at least 10 business days after deliberation.<br>Operational: day-to-day items. No food. Catering for meetings and hot plates count as event items.<br>Conference: registration, travel, and venue only. No food.<br>Collaboration: 2 to 4 clubs, one shared budget listing what each club covers per item, every club's rep at deliberation, same rules as events. | EVT, OPS, COL |
| **Never funded** | Prizes, giveaways, raffles, and gift cards; photographers and photobooths.<br>Fundraisers, charities, donations, and philanthropy; political campaigning.<br>Scholarships, salaries, and paying the club's own members to perform.<br>Events that already happened or fall within 10 business days of deliberation. | EVT-08 to EVT-15, PRO |
| **Deadlines** | Submit at least 5 business days before deliberation. Dates are posted on eLearning and WSAAC's Instagram, not the website.<br>Funding letters arrive within 5 business days after deliberation. Conditional fixes are due within 24 hours.<br>Appeals: email both the Director and Assistant Director within 10 business days. | CYC, APP-02 |
| **When money expires** | Event, collaboration, and conference: right after the event or trip.<br>Operational: the last day of the semester it was issued.<br>Start-up: the end of the academic year. Expired money cannot be reimbursed. | PAY-11, OTH-01 |
| **Getting paid** | Every payment starts with the Allocation Distribution Request Form.<br>Either OSE pays directly, or a member is reimbursed with an itemized receipt and proof of payment.<br>Receipts are due within 10 calendar days after the event; sales tax you paid is reimbursed; reimbursement takes 3 to 6 weeks. | PAY-01 to PAY-11 |
| **The bylaws** | Cover appeals, conflicts of interest, deadlines, strikes, and sanctions.<br>Valid reasons for denial include late or incomplete proposals, items unrelated to the club's mission, events too soon or already past, not being registered with OSE, and banned items. | STR, APP |

**Sources:** WSAAC Fall 2026 Bylaws (passed September 9, 2026), the Allocations Workshop Steps 1 to 7 on eLearning, and OSE's reply on reimbursement (October 5, 2026). When the bylaws change, update this guide and the domain file in the same pull request.
