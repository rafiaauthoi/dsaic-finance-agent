### Quick facts

- **Money source:** the Student Assessment Fee (SAF), $62.00 per student per semester for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027, paid by undergraduate and graduate students. [Web: wmich.edu cost of attendance]
- **Yearly cap:** $6,500 per RSO per fiscal year across all categories, described as a dynamic cap based on the SAF WSAAC takes in and the RSOs it serves; collaboration is a separate $1,500. [Bylaws F26 §6.03(a)] [Cody]
- **Fiscal year:** July 1 to June 30. Caps and strikes reset on it. [Bylaws F26 §5.02(a)]
- **Rounds:** one dropbox and one deliberation per month, with at least three deliberations each semester. The September 2026 bylaws set no limit on how many months an RSO may apply; the older two-cycles rule is gone from the text, though Cody reports it in practice (OQ-01). [Step 3] [Bylaws F26 §5.01] [Cody]
- **Outside funding:** every proposal must include proof the RSO has sought other money (fundraising, dues, sponsorship outreach, or a written statement of attempts). Missing it is grounds for denial. [Bylaws F26 §5.04(g), 7.07(e)]
- **Rhythm:** proposal due (Cody: first Friday), deliberation the next Friday, funding letter about a week later. [Cody] [Step 5 schedule example]
- **Lead time:** an event or conference must be at least 10 business days after the deliberation. [Step 2, Art. 6.02, 6.03]
- **Money never touches the club:** everything is paid by reimbursement or by OSE directly, against the funding letter. [Step 7, Art. 8]
- **Receipts:** reimbursement documents to OSE within 10 days after the event or conference; operational paperwork by the last day of the semester the funding was issued. Reimbursement takes 3 to 6 weeks. [Bylaws F26 §7.05(a), 8.03] [Step 7]
- **Food:** events only, on campus only, max $20 per expected attendee; never for operational or conference. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **Branding:** WSA, SAF Funded and WSAAC logos on funded promo materials and at the event; public ExperienceWMU event link emailed within 3 business days of deliberation. [Step 6, Art. 6, 9]
- **Wording:** keep item names general enough to survive a change of plan ("Bags", not "Custom Embroidery Backpack"). [Cody]

## 2. How the money moves

The whole system in one line: students pay the SAF, WSAAC allocates a portion of it proposal by proposal, the RSO gets a funding letter, and then OSE either reimburses a member or pays the vendor itself, checking every receipt against that letter. [Step 2] [Step 5] [Step 7]

1. **Student pays the SAF.** WSAAC allocates a portion of the SAF to qualified RSOs. [Step 5] [Web: wmuwsa.org/allocations]
2. **RSO submits a proposal** for a specific category (event, operational, conference, collaboration) through the eLearning dropbox before the monthly due date. [Step 3, Art. 5.04] [Step 4 instr]
3. **WSAAC deliberates** and decides item by item. It can fund in full, partially fund, fund conditionally, or deny. [Step 2, Art. 6.01] [Step 3, Art. 5.04]
4. **Funding letter** is issued by the Director and Assistant Director with the approved items and amounts. [Step 5, Art. 8]
5. **An on-campus SAF account** is set up or credited for the allocated items only. [Handbook 24-25]
6. **RSO spends** within the approved items and the category's expiration window; a member is reimbursed or OSE pays directly. [Step 7, Art. 8]
7. **OSE reconciles** receipts and documentation against the approved items. Missing or mismatched paperwork delays or forfeits the money. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]
8. **Leftovers are swept.** SAF money remaining after the event is returned to WSAAC; unused or expired funds go back to the Commission. [Handbook 24-25] [Bylaws F26 §5.02(e)]

### Ground rules that shape everything

- **MON-01** Approved allocations are never issued directly to the RSO. Funds are accessed only through OSE. [Step 7, Art. 8; OSE Spending Guide]
- **MON-02** Yearly cap: $6,500 per RSO per fiscal year across all categories. Collaboration funding has its own separate $1,500 cap. [Step 2, Art. 6.01, 6.03] [Cody]
- **MON-03** Funding is supplemental. It is not meant to fully fund an activity, it depends on remaining SAF funds and Commission approval, and WSAAC reserves the right to partially fund or deny. Decisions are based on eligibility, justification and compliance. [Step 2, Art. 6.01, 6.02]
- **MON-04** Nothing bought before deliberation is covered. The affirmation on every form says no requested items have been purchased yet. [Step 2, Art. 6] [Templates F26]
- **MON-05** The Commission will not allocate funds to recover from bad debt or erase pre-existing debt, nor cover expenses incurred before deliberation. [Bylaws F26 §6.02(a)]
- **MON-06** Approved money expires if not used in the category's window, and expired money cannot be reimbursed. [Step 7, Art. 8; Spending Guide]
- **MON-07** SAF funds are the only money allowed in an RSO's on-campus account; no deposits except to resolve a deficit, and a negative balance freezes the account and the RSO's privileges. Self-generated money (dues, donations) lives in an off-campus bank account under the RSO's own EIN. [Handbook 24-25]
- **MON-08** Providing false information when seeking reimbursement is referred to WMU Public Safety and the Office of Student Conduct. [Handbook 24-25]

> **In practice (Cody):** Yearly cap of 6.5k. Three types of requests we use: Operational for general semester-round purchases, Conference for planning a trip somewhere, Event for an event on campus grounds. The people who evaluate the funding sheet are separate from the people who approve and make the purchases, so the wording that gets you through deliberation and the wording that lets you buy what you actually need are two different problems (see section 6). [Cody]

## 3. Eligibility and standing

Before any proposal, the RSO has to clear the gate in Bylaws Article 4. Miss one item and the slides say not to proceed to the application at all. [Step 1, Art. 4]

### RSO requirements

1. **ELG-01** Registered with the Office of Student Engagement for the current academic year. [Step 1, Art. 4.01]
2. **ELG-02** No outstanding debt with any WMU department at the time of application. [Step 1, Art. 4.01]
3. **ELG-03** WSAAC eLearning module completed. [Step 1, Art. 4.01]
4. **ELG-04** A WSA Representative in good standing. [Step 1, Art. 4.01]
5. **ELG-05** No outstanding documentation from a previously funded WSAAC event or activity. [Step 1, Art. 4.01]
6. **ELG-06** Not under a sanction that disqualifies the RSO from funding (see STR-04). [Bylaws F26 §4.01(f)] The older limit of two months of applications per semester, with a third after a denial, is not in the September 2026 bylaws. [Bylaws F24 §4.01(g)] [Cody]
7. **ELG-07** Not directly receiving SAF funding through another SAF agency, unless a written agreement (an Assembly-passed bill or formal funding agreement) exists. [Step 1, Guiding Principles §3; Art. 4]

### Assembly representation

- **ELG-08** Good standing with the WSA Assembly is evaluated at the RSO level, not per individual. [Step 1, Art. 4.01(d)]
- **ELG-09** Any member may attend Assembly on the RSO's behalf, and it does not have to be the person doing allocations. [Step 1, Art. 4.01(d)]
- **ELG-10** One individual may represent only one RSO in the Assembly. [Step 1, Art. 4.01(d)]
- **ELG-11** Attendance expectations live in the "Assembly & Good Standing" module. The Speaker and Secretary of the Assembly are the contacts for standing questions. [Step 1] [Step 3] [Assembly]
- **ELG-19** To gain standing, the RSO's representative submits the Intent to Represent form. RSO Assembly Members get voting status once it is submitted; General Assembly Members also need three consecutive meetings. Without the form the RSO has No Standing and is not eligible for allocations. [Assembly]
- **ELG-20** More than two unexcused absences in the academic year puts the RSO in Bad Standing, which blocks allocations. To fix it, attend two consecutive meetings; voting rights return at the third. [Assembly]

### Allocations representation and the quiz

- **ELG-12** Each cycle the RSO designates one person to complete the allocations process, including submission and deliberation. [Step 1, Art. 4]
- **ELG-13** One person may complete the process for only one RSO per cycle, and a passing quiz score applies to only that one RSO for that cycle. [Step 1, Art. 4, 4.01(e)]
- **ELG-14** Complete the eLearning modules and score at least 80% on the current Final Allocations Quiz. Passing confirms understanding; it does not guarantee funding. [Step 1, Art. 4.01(e)] When the bylaws change, the quiz is updated and must be retaken even if the RSO already passed that year, as happened after the September 2026 bylaws revision. [News 09-21]
- **ELG-15** Put the Allocations Representative on the form, not the Assembly rep, unless they are the same person. [Step 1, Art. 4]
- **ELG-16** Advisors, faculty, staff and community members cannot represent the RSO. [Step 3, Art. 5.04] [Bylaws F26 §5.04(b)(i)]
- **ELG-17** Every qualification, including a passing quiz score and good Assembly standing, must already be met when the proposal is submitted. [Bylaws F26 §5.04(a)(i)]
- **ELG-18** The proposal must include documentation that the RSO has actively sought outside funding: fundraising, dues collection, sponsorship outreach, or a written statement of attempts made. WSAAC may deny a request from an RSO that has not looked for outside funding. [Bylaws F26 §5.04(g), 7.07(e)]

### 4.1 Event funding in detail

Eligible expenses include space or facility rental, performer, speaker or facilitator fees, equipment rental (A/V, staging, lighting), event supplies and materials, marketing and promotional materials, and food and refreshments when directly related to the event. Everything must be reasonable and directly related, and the event should be meaningful, inclusive, impactful and unique to the student population. [Step 2, Art. 6.02] Events are expected to be on campus unless a legitimate reason is approved. [Overview]

Hard caps on event items:

- **EVT-01** Food: no more than $20 per expected attendee. [Step 2, Art. 6.03] [Step 3, Art. 5.04]
- **EVT-02** Food and drink are funded only if the event is held on Western's campus. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-03** Student performer: $50 per hour up to $200, and the performer cannot be a member of the RSO. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-04** Non-WMU performer: $1,000 for performer costs including travel, lodging and the performance. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-05** Online events (Zoom speakers, presentations): $500. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-06** Promotional items: branded by the vendor with the RSO logo, no more than $250 per fiscal year, clothing excluded. [Bylaws F26 §6.03(a)(i)(2)(h)] (The Spring 2026 slides said $200.) [Step 2]
- **EVT-07** Off-campus events: $500 per event. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]

The Commission shall not fund:

- **EVT-08** Prizes, giveaways and gift cards. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-09** Photographers, photobooths or videographers. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-10** Scholarships and salaries. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-11** Speakers, performers or artists who are current RSO members. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]
- **EVT-12** Events that occur before 10 business days after the deliberation in which funding was requested. [Step 2, Art. 6.03]

> **In practice (Cody):** Be vague. Use something that gives the general idea of what we need to buy, like "Bags", not something too specific like "Custom Embroidery Backpack". - **APP-03** Bylaw-based decisions are reviewed by the Judicial Council and that decision is final. Non-bylaw decisions go to the WSA Assembly, where a two-thirds majority is needed to overturn. [Step 5, Art. 8]
- **APP-04** The Fall 2024 version routed appeals to wsa_ac_chair@wmich.edu and wsa_adallocate@wmich.edu, through the Judicial Council to an Appeals Council (Chief Justice, Membership Chair, Chief of Finance) with a 10-business-day decision, then to the Assembly. Strikes can be appealed under Art. 8.04 and reset at the end of WMU's fiscal year. ## 9. Spending the money

Approved allocations are not issued to the RSO. Funds are accessed through OSE by reimbursement or by direct university payment, all spending follows university financial procedures, and every payment or reimbursement starts with the Allocation Distribution Request Form (used for reimbursements, credit card purchases, vendor payments, independent contractors and campus department payments). Incomplete forms delay processing. Not all purchases qualify for both routes. [Step 7, Art. 8; OSE Spending Guide]

### 9.1 Route 1: pay up front, get reimbursed

- **PAY-01** An RSO member pays out of pocket and submits: an itemized receipt, proof of payment, and documentation matching the approved items. If proof of payment is missing, a bank statement screenshot is required in addition to the receipt. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide; Art. 8]
- **PAY-02** Deadline: reimbursement documents to OSE within 10 days after the event or conference; for operational funding, by the last day of the semester it was issued. Missed deadlines can bring strikes and sanctions. The Spring 2026 slides said 10 days from the purchase date, so submitting soon after buying keeps both readings satisfied. [Bylaws F26 §7.05(a), 8.03] [Step 5, Art. 8]
- **PAY-03** Typical processing time: 3 to 6 weeks. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide] [Cody]
- **PAY-04** Send questions, receipts and documents to the OSE Program Manager at ose-financials@wmich.edu. [Step 7]
- **PAY-05** Only a designated Student Financial Manager (an officer) may sign for and spend RSO account funds. [Handbook 24-25]

> **In practice (Cody):** Reimbursement is straightforward: buy things on your own card, save receipts, submit them in a form. It works well if you are a student worker because you get direct deposit; otherwise you receive a check. The main downside is using your own money and waiting 3 to 6 weeks to see it back. But it is what you need when purchasing quickly with no time to coordinate. [Cody]

> **Note:** The Step 5 slide gives the OSE finance address as ose_finance@wmich.edu; the Step 7 slide gives ose-financials@wmich.edu; the handbook also lists the Program Manager's direct address. Confirm which shared mailbox is live before baking either into a template or a tool (OQ-05). [Step 5] [Step 7] [Handbook 24-25]

### 9.2 Route 2: the university pays directly (OSE credit card or vendor payment)

- **PAY-06** The OSE Program Manager can purchase non-food items online, order groceries for curbside pickup, and pay invoiced vendors on a department credit card. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]
- **PAY-07** Restrictions: no restaurant food purchases, no payments to individuals, no auto-recurring payments. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]
- **PAY-08** The RSO must pick items up during business hours and plan ahead. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]

> **In practice (Cody):** OSE card: you can schedule an appointment with the OSE Program Manager, or send them an Amazon wishlist and they can purchase things for you. [Cody]

### 9.3 Individuals and contractors

**PAY-09** Independent contractors (DJs, speakers, performers, service providers) must be paid by the university, after services are rendered. RSOs may not pay a contractor themselves and seek reimbursement. Required documents: invoice, W-9, and the Independent Contractor Form if applicable. Attempting to pay individuals directly is one of the listed causes of payment delays. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]

### 9.4 Paying WMU departments

**PAY-10** When a WMU department provides the service (facilities, equipment rental, campus services), funds are transferred internally and the Allocation Distribution Request Form is still required. Student Center room charges are handled automatically. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]

### 9.5 The CEAS card

> **In practice (Cody):** If the club is approved for WMU CEAS Funding, you may check out the card after sending your funding letter to the CEAS funding coordinator. Really nice balance: make sure to keep receipts, because they basically do the reimbursement process for you, but let you use their card. [Cody]

> **Note:** Two things to pin down (OQ-06). First, whether the CEAS card spends CEAS money only, or can be used to execute a WSA-approved purchase with the WSA funding letter as the authorization (the note reads like the latter). Second, the exact checkout steps with the CEAS funding coordinator and what they need back afterward. [Inferred]

### 9.6 Expiration and what delays payment

- **PAY-11** Event and collaboration money is valid only for the approved event date and expires when the event concludes. Operational money expires the last day of the semester in which it was issued. Conference money is valid only for the approved dates and expires after travel concludes. Expired money cannot be reimbursed. [Step 7, Art. 8; Spending Guide] [Bylaws F26 §5.02(b)]
- **PAY-12** Unused or expired funds are returned to the Commission; SAF money left in the on-campus account after an event is swept back to WSAAC. [Bylaws F26 §5.02(e)] [Handbook 24-25]
- **PAY-13** Delays come from missing or incorrect documentation, expenses outside the approved categories, late submissions, and attempts to pay individuals directly. OSE's advice: plan early and communicate with them. [Step 7, OSE Spending Guide]
- **PAY-14** RSOs are responsible for following the approved funding terms, submitting documentation on time, and coordinating payments through OSE. [Step 7, Art. 8, 9]
- **PAY-15** Every payment, purchase or reimbursement starts with the Allocation Distribution Request Form. The exceptions are custom items and travel bookings, which are best done in person with the OSE Program Manager. [Spending Guide 25-26]
- **PAY-16** A reimbursement receipt must show the date of purchase, an itemized list, the total, and proof of payment (a zero balance due or the card's last four digits). If the receipt does not prove payment, add a bank screenshot; a bank screenshot on its own is never enough. [Spending Guide 25-26]
- **PAY-17** OSE's card can buy non-food items online (shipped to campus, picked up at OSE) and groceries for curbside pickup, but never restaurant food. For restaurants, either pay and get reimbursed, or use a restaurant that invoices the university. OSE lists past ones, including Condado Taco, Cottage Inn, Jets Pizza, Qdoba, Papa John's and Jimmy John's. [Spending Guide 25-26]
- **PAY-18** Order early. Campus deliveries take longer than home deliveries and the campus mail room does not receive packages on weekends. [Spending Guide 25-26]
- **PAY-19** A current WMU student, faculty or staff member, or a recent WMU employee doing similar work, is not an independent contractor. Paying them goes through Human Resources, not the contractor process. This also matches EVT-11 on RSO members as performers. [Spending Guide 25-26]
- **PAY-20** When a campus department provides a service (custodial, room rental, public safety), a Service Agreement Form is needed along with the distribution request. [Spending Guide 25-26]

### 9.7 Tracking what was spent

- **TRK-01** WSAAC's RSO Budget Tracker has one row per approved budget item: the item, the Amount Approved from the funding letter, up to five receipt columns, and an Amount Remaining that subtracts the receipts. A total at the bottom shows what is left across the allocation. This is the format the project's purchase-tracking phase should follow. [Budget Tracker]
